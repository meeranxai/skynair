Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 10, 2026

Welcome to Skynaire ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information

When you register for Skynaire, we collect:

  • Account Information: Email address, username, display name, profile picture
  • Profile Data: Bio, location (optional), website (optional)
  • Authentication Data: Password (encrypted), Google Sign-In credentials

1.2 Content You Create

  • Articles and posts you publish
  • Comments and replies
  • Stories (automatically deleted after 24 hours)
  • Images and media you upload
  • Messages you send (encrypted)

1.3 Usage Information

  • Device information (model, OS version)
  • App usage statistics
  • IP address and location data (approximate)
  • Crash reports and error logs

1.4 Social Interactions

  • Users you follow and who follow you
  • Posts you like and save
  • Users you block
  • Comments and engagement

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide Services: Enable core app functionality and features
  • Personalization: Customize your feed and recommendations
  • Communication: Send notifications about activity and updates
  • Security: Protect against fraud, abuse, and security threats
  • Improvement: Analyze usage to improve app performance
  • Compliance: Meet legal obligations and enforce our terms

3. Information Sharing

3.1 Public Information

The following information is publicly visible:

  • Your display name and username
  • Profile picture and bio
  • Public posts and articles
  • Comments on public posts
  • Follower and following counts

3.2 We Do NOT Share

  • Your email address
  • Your password
  • Private messages
  • Saved posts
  • Blocked users list

3.3 Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Firebase: Authentication, database, and storage
  • Supabase: Media storage and processing
  • Google Sign-In: Optional authentication method

4. Data Storage and Security

4.1 Security Measures

  • End-to-end encryption for messages
  • Secure HTTPS connections
  • Password hashing and encryption
  • Regular security audits
  • Firestore security rules

4.2 Data Retention

  • Active Accounts: Data retained while account is active
  • Deleted Accounts: Data removed within 30 days
  • Stories: Automatically deleted after 24 hours
  • Backup Data: Retained for 90 days for recovery

5. Your Rights and Choices

5.1 Account Control

  • Access: View and download your data
  • Edit: Update your profile information
  • Delete: Permanently delete your account
  • Export: Request a copy of your data

5.2 Privacy Settings

  • Make your account private
  • Control who can follow you
  • Block unwanted users
  • Manage notification preferences
  • Control data sharing

5.3 Content Control

  • Delete your posts and comments
  • Remove tags from posts
  • Report inappropriate content
  • Hide posts from your feed

6. Children's Privacy

Skynaire is intended for users aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will delete it immediately.

Parents or guardians who believe their child has provided information should contact us at: privacy@skynaire.com

7. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and stored on servers located outside your country. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place to protect your data in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

8. Cookies and Tracking

We use the following technologies:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for app functionality
  • Analytics: To understand app usage (anonymized)
  • Preferences: To remember your settings

You can control cookie preferences in your device settings.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through:

  • In-app notifications
  • Email notifications
  • App update notes

Continued use of Skynaire after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Data Deletion

How to Delete Your Account:

  1. Open Skynaire app
  2. Go to Settings → Account Settings
  3. Select "Delete Account"
  4. Confirm deletion

Upon deletion:

  • Your profile will be immediately deactivated
  • Your posts will be removed within 24 hours
  • Your data will be permanently deleted within 30 days
  • Some data may be retained for legal compliance

11. Your Legal Rights (GDPR/CCPA)

If you are in the EU or California, you have additional rights:

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of your data
  • Right to Rectification: Correct inaccurate data
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your data
  • Right to Restriction: Limit how we use your data
  • Right to Portability: Transfer your data to another service
  • Right to Object: Object to certain data processing

To exercise these rights, contact us at: privacy@skynaire.com
